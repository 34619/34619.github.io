Movement

WASD / Arrow Keys / D-Pad to move

Click/tap canvas to move toward direction

Combat

Walk into enemies to fight

1=Attack, 2=Power Up, 3=Defend, 4=Flee

Power Up: Next attack deals 1.5x damage

Defend: Take half damage this turn

Survival

🍖 Hunger: -2 per step, 0 = lose HP

💧 Thirst: -3 per step, 0 = lose HP

🌡️ Temp: -0.5 per step, 0 = lose HP

Kill enemies, collect loot, craft gear

Defeat bosses every 2 floors
